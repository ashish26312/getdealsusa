About GetDealsUSA

GetDealsUSA collects holiday deals from major US stores such as Best Buy, Walmart, Target, Kohl's, Home Depot, Macy's and Nordstrom Rack, and puts them in one place you can search and filter.

How we pick deals

  • We check each deal on the store's own product page: the current price, the store's listed regular or comparison price, and whether it is in stock.
  • We only list items sold by the store itself (or the brand's official store) and at least 10% below the store's listed price.
  • We re-check prices every other day and remove deals that sell out, end, or are no longer discounted.

What "% off" and our verdicts mean

The % off compares with each store's listed regular or comparison price, which is not always what the item recently sold for. As we collect daily prices, we compare today's price with the item's own price history and label it (for example "Great price" or "Wait"). Until we have about a week of our own history, the label shows how deep the cut is against the store's listed price ("Big discount" is 40% or more, "Solid discount" 20 to 39%, "Modest discount" under 20%), with a rule-of-thumb take: "Buy now" for big cuts, "Good buy" for solid ones, and "Maybe wait" for modest ones, since small holiday cuts often get deeper by Black Friday or Cyber Monday. These takes are guidance, not a promise about future prices. Where we found dated public price reports from before the sale, we use them as estimated history, so a deal can get a full verdict sooner.

Prices change

Stores change prices and stock quickly. Always confirm the price on the store's page before you buy.
