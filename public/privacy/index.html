Privacy Policy | GetDealsUSA

Effective November 20, 2026

This policy explains what information GetDealsUSA (getdealsusa.com, "we") collects and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Email address, if you sign up for deal alerts.
  • Your email and message, if you email us.
  • Technical data that our hosting provider records automatically when you visit, such as IP address, browser type and pages requested. It is used to run and secure the site.
  • Saved deals and recently viewed items are stored only in your own browser (local storage). They are not sent to us. You can clear them by clearing your browser's site data.

How we use it

  • To send the deal alerts you asked for. Every email includes a way to unsubscribe.
  • To answer your messages.
  • To keep the site working and secure, and to understand which pages are useful.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

Who we share it with

Only service providers that help us run the site, under their own privacy terms: our host (Netlify) and, when we send alerts, our email provider. We may disclose information if required by law.

Links to stores and cookies

Deals link to store websites, which have their own privacy policies. Some links may be affiliate links; when you click one, the store or an affiliate network may set cookies to record that you came from us. We do not currently use advertising cookies or analytics; if we add them, we will update this policy first.

How long we keep it

We keep your email address until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it, and contact messages for as long as needed to handle your request.

Your choices and rights

You can unsubscribe at any time. Depending on where you live (for example California and other US states), you may have the right to know, access, correct or delete your personal information. To make a request, email us at getdealsusa.help@gmail.com with the subject "Privacy request". We will not treat you differently for using these rights.

Children

This site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above. Questions? Contact us.
